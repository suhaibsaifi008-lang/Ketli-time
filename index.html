The little details, clearly explained.

What is inside each blend?

Each tea combines four botanicals. Explore every ingredient, its flavour and the complete front-and-back label on the individual tea page.

Do these blends contain caffeine?

The six proposed recipes use flowers, herbs, spices and fruit rather than tea leaves. They are designed as caffeine-free herbal blends.

Can I buy these teas?

We’re restocking soon. Explore the six blends and keep your favourites in your bag while you wait. Restock notification sign-ups are coming soon. This remains an academic brand concept; prices are illustrative and no payment or order is taken.

What do the benefits mean?

Benefits describe flavour, refreshment, warmth and everyday tea rituals. These imagined blends have not been tested for medical effects. The website is an academic marketing concept.

Can I brew inside the paper kettle?

No. Keep the outer paperboard carton dry. A separate sealed pouch is proposed for the blend; brew in a food-safe cup, teapot or infuser.

Are the packaging and ingredients final?

These are concept recipes and original packaging visualisations for a marketing project. Supplier details, physical materials and food-contact suitability would need verification before a real launch.
