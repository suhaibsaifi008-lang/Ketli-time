Six imagined blends. One marketing project.

Ketli Time is an academic brand concept featuring Mehak rose petal, Aanch black pepper, Taazgi cinnamon, Rahat ginger, Sukoon tulsi and Roohani hibiscus. Each proposed recipe combines four botanicals. Full ingredient lists, flavour contributions and brewing suggestions appear on the individual tea pages.

The benefits describe taste, warmth, refreshment and everyday rituals. The recipes have not been tested for therapeutic effects. They are not treatments or medical advice. Check ingredients for individual sensitivities.

A little time before the next batch.

All packs are shown as 50 g at a fictional ₹299 each. Quantities range from one to ten packs per tea. These figures are project illustrations rather than commercial offers.

Add blends to your bag and follow “Restock updates” to the restocking notice. The page preserves your chosen teas and does not complete an order or clear your bag. Restock notification sign-ups are coming soon; no email addresses are collected and no alerts are sent. There is no payment provider, customer account, delivery arrangement, stock reservation or order submission.

Real selling would require confirmed recipes and materials, appropriate food information, supplier and business details, customer-care arrangements and genuine delivery, cancellation and return terms. None are invented for this demonstration.

A little kettle, clearly labelled.

Original generated packaging concepts preserve the brand’s botanical identity. Six informational panels per tea explain the product, ingredients, benefits, serving moment, front/back labels and construction. Ingredient styling images and packaging visualisations are illustrative, not evidence of manufactured products or verified suppliers.

The outer kettle is proposed as folded paperboard with a carry handle and spout. A separate sealed food-safe pouch is proposed inside. Materials, seals and food-contact suitability would need validation for production. Keep the carton dry; never pour water into it.

The readable text on the tea pages is the source of truth for the academic recipes and labels.

Your choices stay on your device.

Favourites use browser storage under ketli-saved. The demo shopping bag uses ketli-demo-cart-v1. Quiz choices, brewing timers and bag selections run in your browser. No name, email, address, card information or quiz response is collected or submitted.

Clear favourites using “Manage saved teas”, empty the shopping bag, or clear browser site data. Visiting the restock page preserves the cart. If storage is unavailable, selections work for the current visit only.

Fonts, images and scripts are served locally. No advertising tracker or external checkout service is included. Hosting systems may keep normal connection logs. Downloading images and tea lists saves files only when you choose; sharing a tea link uses the clipboard or a manual-copy fallback.
